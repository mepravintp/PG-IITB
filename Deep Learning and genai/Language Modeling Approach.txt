Seq2Seq
An encoder–decoder RNN architecture that maps an input sequence to an output sequence of a different length
.
It enables the generation of variable-length outputs, such as those needed for machine translation
.
Method: An encoder compresses input into a single vector, which the decoder then uses to generate output step by step. <br> Example: Translating an English sentence into French
.
It faces an information bottleneck because the entire input must be compressed into one vector, losing detail in long sentences
.
Attention
A mechanism allowing a decoder to focus on specific relevant encoder states instead of relying on a single fixed vector
.
It resolves the Seq2Seq information bottleneck and improves the handling of long-range dependencies
.
